I wanted one on my wall, and I didn't want the one from the shop.

So I designed it myself. SolidWorks for the parts that need to fit together, Blender for the shapes that need to look right. Then I split it into printable parts, numbered every one of them and drew an exploded view so future me would know where everything goes.

## No lights, no motors

Just paint. Primer, base coats, the red stripes, then weathering. That means panel lines, dirt and scorch marks, so it looks like it's done a few runs instead of coming straight out of a box. The weathering took longer than the printing.

## Numbers

About 40 cm wingspan and 50 cm long. Big enough that the detail reads from across the room, small enough to still fit on a wall.
